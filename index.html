WHO WE ARE

Amara Shwe Pyi (ASP)

Amara Shwe Pyi (ASP) is a trading company primarily providing Importer of Record (IOR) and Exporter of Record (EOR) services in Myanmar and Thailand, including cross-border services.

ASP obtains vendor logistics services from GSP Myanmar and GSP Thailand. Gandamar Shwe Pyi (GSP) is a key related intercompany partner of ASP.

OUR LOGISTICS PARTNER

Gandamar Shwe Pyi (GSP)

Gandamar Shwe Pyi (GSP) provides one-stop logistics services and procurement management of record services in Myanmar and Thailand.

GSP's comprehensive services are organized into three distinct divisions:

  • GSP Logistics
  • GSP Procurement
  • GSP Global Support
